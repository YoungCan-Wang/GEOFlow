# WyckoffAgent Finance Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist before approving or publishing any GEOFlow article about WyckoffAgent.

## Entity Clarity

- The article names `WyckoffAgent` in the first paragraph.
- It defines WyckoffAgent as an open-source Wyckoff trading agent or AI stock screener.
- It links to the homepage and GitHub repository.
- It includes at least one exact target phrase, such as `Wyckoff trading agent`, `AI trading agent`, or `stock screener agent`.

## Helpful Search Structure

- The title matches a real search intent.
- H2/H3 headings are question-like or task-like.
- The article includes a short TL;DR.
- The article contains at least one table, list, or FAQ section.
- The article links to at least one related static page or canonical product page.

## Compliance

- No guaranteed-return language.
- No claims of prediction certainty.
- No fake testimonials, fake user counts, or unverifiable performance claims.
- No performance numbers unless they are sourced and accompanied by methodology, limitations, fees, slippage, and risk context.
- Required risk disclosure is present.

## Product Accuracy

- Mentions CLI, web app, local dashboard, or MCP only when relevant.
- Does not describe the project as a fully automated execution system.
- Does not claim support for markets or broker integrations that are not implemented.
- Explains that outputs require human review.

## Final Pass

- Meta description is under 160 characters.
- Slug contains the primary keyword.
- Article is readable without relying on images.
- Internal links point to working URLs.
